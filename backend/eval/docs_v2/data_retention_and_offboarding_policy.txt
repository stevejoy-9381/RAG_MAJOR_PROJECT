DocMind Tenant Data Lifecycle & Offboarding Policy
Document ID: POL-DATA-RET-2026

Section 1: Scope
This policy governs data handling upon corporate subscription termination or contract expiration.

Section 2: Customer Export Window
Departing enterprise customers have 30 calendar days following contract termination to export all indexed documents, vector embeddings, and conversation histories via the automated export API.

Section 3: Archival Freezing
From Day 31 to Day 45 post-termination, tenant data is frozen in cold storage staging.
No user access or API retrieval is permitted during this staging period.

Section 4: Final Cryptographic Sanitization
On Day 46 post-termination, all persistent storage volumes, FAISS indices, SQLite records, and backups are permanently purged.
Media sanitization adheres strictly to NIST SP 800-88 Rev 1 Guidelines for Media Sanitization using Cryptographic Erase (CE) and physical block overwriting.
