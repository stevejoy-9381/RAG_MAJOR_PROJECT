CloudCorp Employee Continuous Learning & Conference Stipend Policy

Section 1: Annual Professional Development Budget
All full-time employees are eligible for an annual professional development and continuous education stipend.
- Technical Engineering Staff (Software, ML, DevOps, QA): $2,500 per calendar year. Eligible expenses include technical conference tickets, cloud certification exams (AWS, GCP, Azure), and professional book purchases.
- Sales, Marketing, and Operations Staff: $1,000 per calendar year for relevant functional training and workshops.
